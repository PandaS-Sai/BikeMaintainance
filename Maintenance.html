Triumph Scrambler 400 X

Periodic Cleaning & Technical Care Protocol
Carnival Red Edition

Hardware Caution Zones

Cluster & TFT

Low pressure only. Keep USB-C seal closed. Never blast switches directly.

Radiator Core

Clean front-to-back strictly. No angled high-pressure on thin aluminum cooling fins.

Cat-Con & Pipe

Cool down for 45 min before washing. Prevent thermal shock & gasket warping.

Drive Chain

Kerosene or dedicated chain spray only. Never use petrol or wire brushes on O-rings.

Monoshock Shaft

Zero high-pressure water near the shock seal. Shield seal head from chain lube.

Maintenance Checklist

Wipe Inverted Fork Stanchions Daily
Tool: Dry microfiber | Removes dust rings and insect debris to protect oil seals.
Clean Headlamp & Mirrors Daily
Tool: Damp towel | Cleans round LED glass and rear-view mirrors for clear night vision.
Body Panels Two-Bucket Wash Weekly
Tool: pH-neutral soap + wash mitt | Hand-wash tank, frame, and wheels. Dry with plush towel.
Fluid Level Sight Check Weekly
Check engine oil window (bike upright) and verify coolant reservoir marker.
Deep Chain Degrease Bi-Weekly
Tool: Grunge brush + Kerosene | Engine OFF, bike in neutral. Scrub gunk off links; dry with rag.
Chain Lube Application Bi-Weekly
Tool: O-Ring chain lube | Spray inside run of warm rollers; let cure 4–6 hrs before riding.
Clean Monoshock Cavity Monthly
Tool: Soft bottle brush + soap | Clean spring coils & lower linkage; gently rinse with low pressure.
Radiator Fin Flush Monthly
Tool: Diluted APC + water spray | Flush trapped grit and bugs from cooling fins front-to-back.
Carnival Red Tank UV Protection Quarterly
Tool: Carnauba wax or synthetic sealant | Buff clear coat to guard against swirl marks and UV sun fade.
Fasteners & Frame Anti-Corrosion Quarterly
Tool: ACF-50 or anti-corrosion spray | Wipe exposed bolt heads, footpeg pins, and subframe joints.
