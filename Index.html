Triumph Scrambler 400 X

Care & Maintenance Checklist
Carnival Red

Hardware Caution Zones

Cluster & TFT

Low pressure only. Keep USB-C seal closed.

Radiator Core

Clean front-to-back only. Never bend fins.

Cat-Con & Exhaust

Cool down 45 mins before applying water.

Drive Chain

Kerosene or dedicated chain spray only.

Monoshock Shaft

Zero direct pressure jets near the shock wiper.

Maintenance Checklist

Wipe Fork Stanchions Daily
Dry microfiber | Clean dust rings off gold stanchions to save seals.
Clean Headlamp & Mirrors Daily
Damp cloth | Clean road film from LED glass and mirrors.
Two-Bucket Panel Wash Weekly
pH-neutral soap | Hand-wash tank, frame, and wheels. Dry thoroughly.
Chain Clean & Lubrication Bi-Weekly
Kerosene + O-ring lube | Degrease chain, dry, and spray inner roller run.
Clean Monoshock Cavity Monthly
Soft brush + low pressure rinse | Clean spring and lower linkage.
